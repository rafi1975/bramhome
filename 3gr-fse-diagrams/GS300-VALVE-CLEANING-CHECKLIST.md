# 2006 Lexus GS300 (GRS190) — Intake Valve Cleaning Checklist
## Engine: 3GR-FSE | Ireland RHD | Holts EGR & Carb Cleaner Spray

Use this only with the **intake manifold already removed**.  
Do **not** run the engine until valve covers and intake are back on.

---

## RHD layout (stand at front of car)

```
        YOU (front grille)
              ↓
  LEFT bay              RIGHT bay
  PASSENGER             DRIVER
  Bank 2                Bank 1
  Cyl 2, 4, 6           Cyl 1, 3, 5
```

- Cylinder **#1** = driver side, **front** pair of twin oval ports  
- Each cylinder = **one opening split by a bridge into two ovals**  
- Clean **both ovals** per cylinder when that cylinder’s intake valves are closed  

Firing order: **1-2-3-4-5-6**

---

## Tools / materials

- [ ] 22 mm socket + long ratchet / breaker bar (crank pulley bolt)
- [ ] Inspection camera
- [ ] Holts EGR & Carb Cleaner Spray 500 ml
- [ ] Shop vacuum (or strong handheld vac)
- [ ] Plastic picks / nylon brush / old toothbrush
- [ ] Painter’s tape or duct tape
- [ ] Gloves, eye protection
- [ ] Lint-free rags
- [ ] All 6 spark plugs removed (strongly recommended)

---

## Before you start

- [ ] Battery disconnected if you prefer (not required for hand-turning)
- [ ] All **6 spark plugs OUT**
- [ ] Tape over every intake port you’re **not** working on
- [ ] Cover open cams if valve covers are off
- [ ] Holts spray ready — short bursts only, don’t flood

---

## How to know valves are CLOSED

Use **both** checks:

### A) Cam (if valve covers off)
- Intake cam = closer to the **valley / centre**
- For that cylinder: lobe on **base circle** (round part on lifter)
- Pointy part of lobe **not** pushing down → CLOSED

### B) Camera looking **down** into the twin ovals
| CLOSED — clean | OPEN — turn crank more |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Two round valve discs **block** the holes | Dark **gap** deeper into cylinder |
| Can’t see into the chamber | Path into chamber is open |

Diagram: `valve-closed-vs-open.png`

---

## Turning the crank

1. 22 mm socket on **crank pulley bolt** (front of engine)
2. Turn **CLOCKWISE only** (top of pulley moves to the **right**)
3. Do **not** turn anti-clockwise (can loosen the bolt)
4. Turn slowly until that cylinder’s intake valves are CLOSED
5. Confirm with camera before spraying

Diagram: `crank-rotation-clockwise.png`

## Per-cylinder procedure (repeat ×6)

Suggested order: **#1 → #2 → #3 → #4 → #5 → #6**

### For each cylinder

1. [ ] Identify the **two ovals** for this cylinder
2. [ ] Tape **all other ports**
3. [ ] Turn crank clockwise until intake valves CLOSED (cam + camera)
4. [ ] Shake Holts can; spray **short bursts** into both ovals
5. [ ] Wait **5–10 minutes**
6. [ ] Plastic pick / nylon brush — scrape carbon gently  
      - Get port walls, valve backs, and around the stem if reachable  
      - Do **not** gouge aluminum or valve seats with metal picks
7. [ ] **Vacuum constantly** while scraping
8. [ ] Spray again lightly if needed; scrape; vacuum
9. [ ] Camera check — ports as clean as practical; **no loose grit**
10. [ ] Wipe dry; **tape those two ovals shut**
11. [ ] Move to next cylinder — turn crank again for its CLOSED position

### Critical rules

- Never clean with valves **OPEN**
- Never turn the crank while a puddle of cleaner is sitting in a port
- Never leave Holts soaking on rubber hoses / wiring
- Vacuum out liquid — don’t let a lot fall past the valves into the cylinder

---

## After all 6 cylinders

- [ ] All ports clean and taped or covered
- [ ] No rags, picks, or tape pieces left in ports
- [ ] Fix oil leaks (banjo washers, etc.) while still open if planned
- [ ] Install **new valve cover gaskets** + spark plug tube seals  
      - RTV **only** at the 4 corners where cover meets timing cover  
      - Torque: 10 mm bolts **7 ft-lb (10 N·m)** / 12 mm bolts **15 ft-lb (21 N·m)**
- [ ] Wait ~2 hours after RTV before first start (Toyota-style cure)
- [ ] New **intake manifold gasket** when plenum goes back on
- [ ] Reinstall coils, plugs, connectors, vacuum lines
- [ ] Oil + coolant levels OK before first start
- [ ] Fresh fuel later (car sat 5 years) — don’t use old petrol

---

## First start notes (later — needs power)

- Expect smoke / smell if residual cleaner/carbon burns off
- Check banjo / timing-cover seam for **fresh** oil
- Change oil soon after first runs if a lot of cleaner entered cylinders
